Bloom or Boom Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

The short version

  • Bloom or Boom has no accounts. We never ask for your name, email or phone number to play.
  • Your progress stays on your device.
  • Purchases go through Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your card details.
  • We don't sell your personal information.
  • The Game shows no ads and sends no gameplay analytics.

1. Who we are

Bloom or Boom (the "Game") is made by 2E Games ("we", "us"). We are responsible for the personal information described here. Contact us at 2egames@gmail.com.

2. What stays on your device

The Game saves your progress, settings, coins, items and purchase history on your device. We don't receive a copy.

  • Language and country. The Game reads your phone's language and region settings, on the device, to choose its language and the holiday for Stage 1. This isn't sent to us.
  • Device backup. If backup is turned on for your device, Android (or iOS) may back up the Game's saved data to your Google (or Apple) account. That backup is run by Google or Apple under their own privacy policies, and we can't see it.

3. What we collect and why

Today we only receive information when you buy something or contact us.

4. Who we share information with

We don't sell your personal information or share it for targeted advertising. We share it only:

  • with the service providers named in this policy, who handle it for us under contract (today: RevenueCat, and Google or Apple for payments);
  • when the law requires it, or to protect the rights and safety of players, the public or us;
  • with a buyer or successor if the Game or our company is sold or merged, under this policy's protections.

5. How long we keep it

  • On your device: until you delete the Game or its data.
  • Purchase records: as long as needed to deliver and restore purchases, handle refunds and meet tax and accounting rules.
  • Support emails: up to 2 years after the conversation ends.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your game data: uninstall the Game, or clear its data in your phone's settings. Device backups are managed in your Google or Apple account.
  • Ask about data we hold: email 2egames@gmail.com to see, correct, delete or get a copy of your purchase records or support emails. Because there are no accounts, include your store order number (Google Play) or receipt so we can find your records.
  • EU and UK: you can also restrict or object to our use of your information, withdraw consent at any time, and complain to your local data protection authority.
  • California and other US states: you have the right to know, delete and correct your information, and to opt out of its sale or sharing. We don't sell or share it, and we won't treat you differently for using your rights.

We reply within 30 days, or sooner if your local law requires it.

7. Children

The Game is for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 where EU law sets a higher age). If you think a child has given us personal information, email 2egames@gmail.com and we'll delete it.

8. Security and international transfers

We and our providers use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, but no system is completely secure. Our providers may process information in the United States and other countries. Where the law requires it, transfers are protected by safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

9. Changes to this policy

We'll update this policy when the Game changes. The date at the top shows the latest version. For important changes, we'll also tell you in the Game.

10. Contact

Questions or requests about your privacy: 2E Games, 2egames@gmail.com.
